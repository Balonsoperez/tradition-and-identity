---
title: "How the posters evolved"
---

The 2000 poster highlights the Centro Cultural de San José, which is significant because it shows that the festival was emphasizing a community space where Mexican culture is celebrated and shared. Unlike the 1995 poster, which focused mainly on traditional symbols like mariachi musicians and Our Lady of Guadalupe, the 2000 poster includes the cultural center itself along with folklórico dancers and mariachi. This change shows a shift toward celebrating not just the traditions, but also the places and community spaces that bring people together to experience and maintain Mexican heritage.
